Live Transcription — Support

Live Transcription turns spoken conversation into large, on-screen captions in real time — fully on your device. Here is how to get the most out of it, and how to reach us.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: email hello@powercoffee.me. We aim to reply within a few days.

Getting started

  • Permissions. On first launch, grant Microphone and Speech Recognition access so the App can caption nearby speech. You can change these at any time in iOS Settings → Live Transcription.
  • Start captioning. Tap the record button and spoken words appear as large captions. Adjust the caption size and choose a high-contrast theme in Settings.
  • Re-reading. Scroll back to re-read; the captions will not auto-scroll away from you. Tap “Jump to live” to return to the latest line.

Higher accuracy & speaker labels

  • Download a model. For higher-accuracy captioning, open Settings → Manage Models and download an on-device speech model. Downloads are user-initiated, show progress, and ask before using cellular data.
  • Speaker labels (Pro). Pro adds on-device speaker recognition that labels who is speaking. Recognition can occasionally mislabel a voice — you can rename a speaker, merge two speakers that are really the same person, or reassign a single line. Download the speaker model in Settings → Manage Models to enable it.

Pro

  • What is included. A one-time purchase unlocks speaker labels, unlimited saved history, and transcript export (readable text or JSON).
  • Restore purchases. Reinstalled, or on a new device? Settings → Restore Purchases re-applies your Pro upgrade. Pro is Family Sharing–enabled.

Privacy

All audio and captions are processed on your device; nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.
