Privacy Policy

For the Live Transcription iOS application.
Effective date: 5 June 2026 · Last updated: 5 June 2026 · Version 1.0

Live Transcription (the "App") processes all audio, captions, and speaker labels entirely on your device. It does not collect, store off your device, or transmit any personal data. There is no analytics, no advertising, no telemetry, and no account that you create with us.

Who we are

The App is published by Sean Power, an individual based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). You can contact us at hello@powercoffee.me. For the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, we are the data controller for any personal data we process in connection with the App — although, as explained below, that processing is limited to direct correspondence you initiate with us.

On-device by design

Live Transcription captions speech in real time using Apple's on-device speech recognition and an optional on-device speech model (WhisperKit). Optional speaker recognition runs an on-device model (FluidAudio). All of this happens on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Microphone audio is captured and transcribed locally. Speech recognition is pinned to on-device processing; your audio is never sent to us or to any server for transcription.
  • Captions, transcripts, and speaker labels are generated and stored on your device only.
  • Saved transcripts remain in the App on your device. If you have iCloud or local device backups enabled, they are included in your own backup (governed by Apple's terms) — they are never sent to us.

What the App does not collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, postal address, phone number, or any other contact details.
  • Your audio recordings or transcripts — they never leave your device.
  • Your IP address, geolocation, or device identifier (IDFA, IDFV, advertising ID).
  • Usage analytics, behavioural data, session recordings, or interaction telemetry.
  • Crash logs sent to third-party crash-reporting services. Any crash data iOS shares with Apple is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and is only ever seen by us in aggregated, anonymised form via App Store Connect, if at all.

What information leaves your device

The App has no backend server — there is no service we operate that receives data from the App. The only network traffic the App makes is initiated by you and goes directly to a third party, never to us:

  • On-device model downloads. For higher-accuracy captioning or speaker recognition, you may choose to download an on-device machine-learning model (Settings → Manage Models). When you do, the App downloads the model files over HTTPS from a model host (Hugging Face). This transfers only the model files to your device; no personal data, audio, or transcripts are sent. The download is always user-initiated, shown with progress, and confirmed before using cellular data.
  • In-app purchase. The optional one-time "Pro" upgrade is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment under Apple's own privacy policy; we never receive your payment details, and we operate no purchase server.

Third parties we do not share with

Because we do not collect personal data, we have no personal data to share, sell, rent, or otherwise transfer to any third party — including advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers, or affiliated companies.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and is designed to assist Deaf and hard-of-hearing users. Because we do not collect any personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has somehow had personal data exposed to us — for example by emailing us — please contact hello@powercoffee.me and we will delete that correspondence.

Correspondence with us

If you email us at hello@powercoffee.me, our email provider will, by necessity, process your email address and the content of your message so that we can read and reply. We retain support email only as long as needed to handle the matter, and in any case no longer than 24 months. The legal basis under Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR is our legitimate interest in providing user support and responding to enquiries.

Your rights under UK GDPR

If you are in the United Kingdom or the EEA, you have the right to access any personal data we hold about you, to have inaccurate data corrected, to have your data erased, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. In practice, the only personal data we hold about you is anything you have voluntarily included in an email to us. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@powercoffee.me and we will respond within one month.

Security

Because the App does not transmit your audio or transcripts to us, the security boundary for your content is the security of your own device. Saved transcripts live in the App's on-device storage and are protected by iOS data protection.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change the App so that it does collect, store, or transmit personal data, we will update this policy before the change ships and post the new version here with a revised effective date. Material changes will be highlighted in the App's release notes. The current version is always available at https://legal.powercoffee.me/live-transcription/privacy/.
